This folder contains genuine, textured Mixamo FBX character assets from the three Basic Shooter Pack ZIP files provided by the user.
Ch15 and Ch35 original 4096px embedded PNG textures were resized to 1024px, reducing download size; FBX skeletal geometry was retained.
SWAT is legacy FBX version 6100 and may require an updated export to load reliably in Three.js.
Animation FBXs are included separately for idle, walk, run, jump, fire, and reload.
